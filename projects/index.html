Research Projects

Generative Models of Code-Mixing
Advisor: Dr. Monojit Choudhury

I am working on building computational models to process code-mixed (or code-switched) language. To overcome the data scarcity, we utilized established linguistic theories to create large corpora of synthetic code-mixed data. In the context of language modeling, we showed that the synthetic data, with appropriate sampling and training curricula, can efficiently complement the limited real code-mixed data (ACL 2018). In our work on learning pre-trained word embeddings, we analyzed the application of existing cross-lingual techniques for code-mixing and showed that instead directly learning embeddings from synthetic CM data is a more suitable approach (EMNLP 2018).

Automatic Short Answer Grading
Advisors: Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharyya, Dr. Shourya Roy

I explored ideas from related NLP problems of, question answering, textual entailment and paraphrase detection. I identified key concepts in the form of contiguous word patterns and used them to compute relevance and support by comparing it with question and reference answer. I utilized pre-trained word embeddings (BoW and dependency-based) to learn pattern vectors as input to vector-based similarity measures. [Report]

Opinion Summarization
Advisor: Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharyya

I worked on understanding the workings of the sub-systems, summarization and sentiment analysis. I also explored the applicability of sub-modular set functions to capture various aspects in the context of multi-document summarization. Built a Hindi text summarizer using TextRank algorithm and a translation system. [Report]

Key Academic Projects

Deep Learning for Sentiment Analysis
Guide: Prof. Sunita Sarawagi

Trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and long short-term memory networks (LSTMs) for the task of sentiment analysis. We experimented with the two variants, one where we randomly initialize the embedding layer and the other where we used GLoVe pre-trained word embeddings. We obtained results close to the state of the art (75%) both in the case of CNN and LSTM on rotten tomatoes movie reviews consisting of 5000 positive and negative reviews each.

Automatic Singer Recognition
Guide: Prof. Preethi Jyothi

We analyzed the performance of gaussian mixture models and neural network models for the task of singer recognition. We curated a dataset of 130 songs spanning 5 male and 5 female singers and extracted the MFCC features. We extensively compared the performance of GMMs and FNNs on this dataset. Some of the challenges involved in this project were vocal/non-vocal segmentation and processing songs with more than one singer.

Predictive Technical Assistant to Online Choice Filling
Guide: Prof. Sharat Chandran

We developed an online portal to assist IIT aspirants through the process of choice filling for seats in IIT’s and NIT’s. We predicted the odds of getting various programs by analyzing the past rank data while considering the personal program and region interests of the student. We launched our application on Amazon AWS and received an overwhelming response from student community with over thousands of sessions.

Star Wars Droids: Modeling, Rendering and Animation
Guide: Prof. Parag Chaudhuri

We developed hierarchical models of dum series pit droid (humanoid) and tri droid (non-humanoid) in entirety using OpenGL. Designed an environment and textured both the droids and environment. Lightened the scene using both directional and spot lights. We also animated the droids by linearly interpolating the rendered key frames and also facilitated for slow-in and slow-out movement of animation.
