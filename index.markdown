Hi, I am a PhD student at the [Language Technologies Institute](https://lti.cs.cmu.edu/), Carnegie Mellon University working with [Prof. Teruko Mitamura](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gjsxBCkAAAAJ). My current research interests are on problems relating to event semantics and multilingual NLP. Prior to my Masters, I was a research fellow at Microsoft Research India, working with [Dr. Monojit Choudhury](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=WR1ImCMAAAAJ) on code-switching related tasks. I graduated with a Bachelor's in Computer Science from IIT Bombay in 2017.
